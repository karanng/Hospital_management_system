🏥 Hospital Management System (HMS)
A comprehensive web-based solution to streamline hospital operations — from patient registration and doctor allocation to appointment scheduling and ward management. This system improves efficiency, accuracy, and patient care, designed using Django and modern web technologies.

🚀 Overview
The Hospital Management System (HMS) is built to simplify and automate the day-to-day processes of hospitals and clinics. It allows administrators, staff, and medical professionals to manage data, resources, and services effectively through a centralized platform.

🎯 Key Features
👤 Patient Management – Register, update, and monitor patient records.

🩺 Doctor Management – Allocate, schedule, and manage doctors efficiently.

📅 Appointment Scheduling – Book and manage appointments with automated reminders.

🏥 Outpatient & Inpatient Management – Organize wards, beds, and patient admissions.

🧑‍⚕️ Staff & Resource Allocation – Optimize staff and medical resource usage.

📊 Administrative Tasks – Handle backend processes like reporting and analytics.

⚙️ Usage Scenarios
Clinics managing a high volume of outpatient visits.

Hospitals tracking inpatients and ward allocations.

Administrative staff needing to streamline resource management.

Healthcare IT teams looking to digitize hospital operations.
